Experiment: Multilingual Technical Reasoning Pilot
Model: Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B
Model source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B
Evaluation environment: Google Colab
Hardware: NVIDIA Tesla T4
Transformers version: 4.51.3
Underlying questions: 10
Language forms: English, Roman Urdu, Urdu script, Code-switched
Prompts: 40

Scoring:
Human-reviewed scores from 0 to 2 for correctness,
meaning preservation, and uncertainty handling.

Limitations:
This is a small exploratory pilot. Translation equivalence,
question selection, subjective scoring, and sampling uncertainty
limit generalization. Results should not be interpreted as a
general ranking of model performance across languages.
